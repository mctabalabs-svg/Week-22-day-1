A contribution starts in the pending state as soon as the cycle opens and remains there until an external event changes it. The only transition to paid is triggered by the M-Pesa callback, which verifies the payment and stamps the paid_at timestamp. If the member initiates payment but M-Pesa reports an error, the contribution moves to failed. A separate cron job runs at the end of the cycle and scans for any contributions still in pending; those are marked overdue. When a contribution becomes overdue, the system writes a new row in the fines table for that member and cycle. Once a contribution reaches a terminal state—paid, failed, or overdue—it never returns to pending.
